JiraTack

Click the toolbar icon on any page to drop pins. No bookmarklet, so it works on sites whose security policy blocks one.

Supported: Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Safari is not supported in this release; use one of those three.

In JiraTack, open a canvas and copy the bookmarklet link you would have dragged to your bookmarks bar. Paste it here once. It carries your token and endpoints; nothing else is needed.
